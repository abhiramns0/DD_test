HOW TO PLAY

GOAL

The island is sinking! Collect 🪵 wood, 🍃 leaves and ⚡ energy, build your vehicle, board it and escape before the water wins.

CONTROLS

Drag to move. Stand next to trees, logs, bushes and crystals to collect. Stand at your station to build, then climb aboard.

MODES

CO-OP — Build one vehicle together. Once someone boards, a 10 s countdown starts — be aboard or get left behind. Solo gets one revive.

TOURNAMENT — Each team escapes island after island. Last team standing wins.

END GAME — Best of 3 rounds on one island. Build first, push or stun rivals, escape first.

POWER-UPS (END GAME)

🎁 Airdrops give a 🛡️ shield (blocks one hit, 5 s) or a rare 🔫 stun gun — everyone inside the funnel is frozen for 3 s.

TEAMWORK

🐊 Crocodiles are deadly. A teammate who stands next to you for 3 s (within 10 s) revives you.
